Hello! I'm Ash!

"Their perception of who I am, won't make me question who I am"
- Aristos Norman Petrou

Hello! My name is Ash, and I’m a 19-year-old artist from Colorado Springs. I’ve loved art for as long as I can remember, and all I’ve ever wanted is to build a career around it. My favorite music artists are $uicideboy$, which is where my favoritequote comes from, but I listen to a lot of other genres too. honestly, too many to count. My work is heavily inspired by my favorite show, Arcane, and my favorite illustrator, Junji Ito. I grew up with a goth mom, so I naturally fell into the alternative aesthetic, and when I actually get the motivation to go out, I’m usually dressed in obnoxiously green cybergoth outfits. My goal isn’t really to make money, I just want to show my art to the world and hopefully make a couple people happy.
